The early days

1996

Winter of 1996, two ski patrollers bought a condemned stone springhouse above Heber Valley because the water under it was too good to leave in the ground. They rigged a copper syrup kettle to a single fill line and sold botanical sodas out of the back door in unlabeled steel cans — spruce tip, prickly pear, whatever the mountain gave them that season.

The bottling works still runs on that same spring. Every soda starts as cold mountain water — real cane sugar, botanicals picked within sight of the building, carbonated on site at six thousand feet.
